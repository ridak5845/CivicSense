# 🏙️ CivicSense – AI-Powered Hyperlocal Problem Solver

> Empowering citizens to report, track, and resolve community issues using Artificial Intelligence.

---

## 📌 Overview

CivicSense is an AI-powered civic engagement platform that bridges the gap between citizens and municipal authorities. It enables users to report local issues such as potholes, water leakages, garbage dumping, damaged streetlights, road damage, and other public infrastructure problems.

Using Google Gemini AI, the platform analyzes uploaded images and videos, identifies the issue, categorizes it, estimates its severity, and automatically generates a structured complaint. CivicSense also promotes transparency, accountability, and community participation through real-time tracking, gamification, AI-powered insights, and a dedicated government dashboard.

---

# 🚀 Problem Statement

Communities frequently face civic issues such as potholes, water leakages, damaged streetlights, waste management concerns, and public infrastructure challenges. Existing reporting systems are often fragmented, difficult to track, and lack transparency.

Citizens rarely receive timely updates, duplicate complaints are common, and municipal authorities struggle to prioritize and resolve issues efficiently.

CivicSense addresses these challenges by providing an intelligent platform where citizens can report, verify, track, and resolve community issues using Artificial Intelligence, geolocation, and community collaboration.

# ⚙️ How It Works

1. User signs into CivicSense.
2. Uploads an image or video of a civic issue.
3. AI analyzes the uploaded media.
4. AI detects the issue category and severity.
5. AI generates a structured complaint.
6. Complaint is submitted to the relevant department.
7. Authorities verify and update the complaint.
8. Citizens track progress in real time.
9. Community members support issues through upvotes.
10. Once resolved, users receive points and badges.
